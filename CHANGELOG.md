## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- **Paper performance ignores cash in and out:** period P&L, the performance chart, and max drawdown no longer treat a deposit, withdrawal, or transfer as profit or loss. `changeAmount` is the equity change minus that external cash. The chart plots account equity with that cash removed. Today's loss limit uses the same split, so moving cash does not trip it or hide a real loss (`GET /api/v1/portfolio/performance`, `docs/features/paper-trading.md`, `docs/features/risk-limits.md`)
- **Similar setups use the candle clock:** a forensics read with `asOf` compares past setups with the tape known by that time. An after-window that had not finished yet stays out. Omit `asOf` and the comparison still uses now. The coin chart and `/forensics` already send `asOf` at the end of a finished move, and the similar list names that clock (`GET /api/v1/market/forensics`, `docs/features/forensics.md`)
- **OKX liquidation size:** a missing or non-positive contract size is dropped on the live socket and on the history page. It is no longer stored as 1 contract, which made a BTC-USDT-SWAP print about 100 times too large. The history window stays uncovered until the size is known. Prints already stored are left as written.

### Changed
- **Resting paper fills store the situation at the fill.** A limit, stop, bracket, or OCO keeps the read from placement on the order and writes a new read on the fill. The trade, the open position, and a situation alert use that fill read. A failed read at the fill stays blank. Each partial fill stores its own read. A market order still stores the read when it fills. Equities still skip it (`docs/features/coin-situation.md`, `docs/features/paper-trading.md`)
- **Combined coin reads stay blank until every asked venue is in.** Futures CVD leaves out a minute that one venue missed instead of filling it from the others. A minute nobody printed is not a gap. Combined open interest, funding pressure, participation, net long/short, positioning, and squeeze risk stay blank and name the missing venue. A partial sum is not shown as the combined number (`GET /api/v1/market/cvd`, `open-interest`, `funding-rate`, `participation`, `net-position`, `positioning`, `squeeze-risk`)

### Added
- **What the fill paid against the quote:** after a crypto market order finishes, trade history shows the fee and the slippage the comparison quoted beside what the fills paid. The difference is actual minus expected, in quote currency. Positive means the fill cost more. Slippage uses the touch from that comparison, before the fee. A spread order shows the line when it is filled or canceled. The quote is kept for two minutes and has to match the order (`executionQuoteId` on `POST /api/v1/portfolio/orders`, `costCheck` on trade history, MCP `compare_order_execution` and `list_portfolio_trades`) (`docs/features/paper-trading.md`)
- **Raise the stop to the buy price after the first target:** an OCO or bracket can move the stop up after the nearest take-profit fills completely. An OCO uses the average cost. A bracket uses the average entry fill. The stop never moves down and stays below the next open target. The preview shows that price (`moveStopToEntry`, `moveStopPrice`) (`docs/features/paper-trading.md`)
- **Scale OCO and bracket exits across take-profit targets:** a paper OCO or bracket can split the size across several take-profit limits. Each `percent` is a share of the size and the shares sum to 100. `offsetPercent` is a gain above the average cost or the bracket entry. When a target fills, the stop quantity becomes the coins still held (`takeProfitTargets` on `POST /api/v1/portfolio/orders/oco`, `POST /api/v1/portfolio/orders/bracket`, and the order preview) (`docs/features/paper-trading.md`)
- **When the order comparison was calculated:** the crypto market ticket shows the clock of the last successful comparison. While a newer read is in flight, including the automatic refresh, the previous rows stay and the ticket says it is updating (`docs/features/paper-trading.md`)
- **Venue size on the order comparison:** each exchange on a compared path shows how much of the size it takes, next to its name (`docs/features/paper-trading.md`)
- **Compare a market order, best cost, and a time split before you place it:** a crypto market ticket shows the fee, the slippage, and the cash for the same size three ways. This venue walks that book now. Best cost walks Binance, Coinbase, and Bybit now. Over time repeats a slice of that walk as if each slice finds this book again. If the book does not refill, over time matches best cost. A path the book cannot hold fills nothing. A slippage cap applies to best cost and over time. Choosing a column is what gets placed. Nothing is written until you place it (`POST /api/v1/portfolio/orders/execution-compare`, MCP `compare_order_execution`) (`docs/features/paper-trading.md`)
- **Spread a best-cost paper order across a clock you choose:** a crypto market order with best cost can fill as timed slices. The ticket sets the minutes and the number of slices, for example 15 slices over 30 minutes or 5 slices over 5 minutes. The default is 10 slices over 10 minutes. The last slice is due when the minutes end. Each slice reads Binance, Coinbase, and Bybit again and takes the cheapest venue for the size still open. The preview shows that schedule, the cash or quantity it locks, and the first slice on the current book. Nothing is written until you place it. Cancel releases what has not filled. The open orders tab lists the working order (`POST /api/v1/portfolio/orders` `spread`, `spreadMinutes`, `sliceCount`, `GET` and `DELETE /api/v1/portfolio/spread-orders`, MCP `preview_portfolio_order`, `place_portfolio_order`, `list_spread_orders`, `cancel_spread_order`) (`docs/features/paper-trading.md`)
- **Portfolio coins that move together:** the paper book shows whether the open coins moved together over the last day, and the day swing of that mix against the swing if they had moved independently. A short offsets a long. A coin without enough shared minutes is named and left out. Pairs are listed from the strongest link, up or down. The order preview says whether that order raises or lowers the swing. A failed read leaves the price in place (`GET /api/v1/portfolio/correlation`, `POST /api/v1/portfolio/orders/preview` `correlation`, MCP `get_portfolio_correlation`) (`docs/features/risk-limits.md`)
- **Replay a past window on the paper book:** the stress preview can take a start and end instead of typed percents. Each open position is repriced by that venue's close-to-close move. A coin on two venues keeps both moves, and the coin line is their value-weighted average. Coins are listed with the largest up or down move first. The equity line shows that money change as a percent of the current book. A replay also draws the book through each close in the window, including a drop that later recovers, and names the lowest point. A position with no close at either end stays unchanged and is named. Cash does not change. Nothing is traded (`POST /api/v1/portfolio/stress` `from`/`to`, MCP and Python `stress_portfolio`, portfolio screen) (`docs/features/risk-limits.md`)
