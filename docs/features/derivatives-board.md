# Derivatives board

## Goal

Rank the liquid USDT perpetuals in one sortable table: mark, funding, open interest, crowd long, top-versus-crowd, day CVD, the current price-versus-CVD run, print size, order count, unusual readings, liquidation heat, bid share, taker buy share, and 24-hour liquidations.

## Behavior

`GET /api/v1/market/derivatives-board` follows the same liquid USDT names as the futures recorder. The recorder keeps 100 names. The board opens on 36 and accepts up to 100. `exchange` is `binance`, `bybit`, `okx`, `hyperliquid`, or `all`.

Each row has:

- Mark price from the fresh venue with the most open interest, and which venue that was.
- Predicted funding. When every venue's open interest is also fresh, the rate is weighted by that open interest. Otherwise the fresh rates are averaged and `fundingWeight` is `equal`.
- Open interest in USDT, plus the change over 1 hour and 24 hours. The 24-hour change uses the stored sample within 90 minutes of 24 hours ago when the live series does not reach that hour. `change24hMissing` names each venue with no sample. That venue is not treated as zero, and the combined cell stays blank. The 1-hour change stays on the live series.
- Crowd long percent: the account long share. When every venue's open interest is also fresh, the shares are weighted by that open interest. Otherwise they are averaged. A 5-minute print older than 10 minutes is old.
- Taker buy share over the last hour. Notionals are summed across venues. An hour that is not complete stays blank.
- Long and short liquidation notional over the last 24 hours.
- Top minus crowd, in percentage points. Bybit does not publish a top-trader ratio, so the combined cell uses the venues that do and names Bybit as unsupported. `gapScope` lists who is inside the number.
- Day CVD: aggressive buy minus sell since 00:00 UTC. The cell stays empty until every expected venue's tape reaches that open and the newest bucket is still fresh.
- Price versus CVD: the current run where price and cumulative delta move opposite ways (`price_up_cvd_down` or `price_down_cvd_up`), and how long that run has lasted. When the tape is definite and there is no current run, the cell says the two are in line (`agrees`). A dash means the tape does not cover the read. The cell needs the tape to reach 00:00 UTC, a price on the newest bar, and a newest minute that is still fresh. One venue shows that venue. Combined uses the summed series, not an average of the venue labels, and stays empty unless every expected venue's 24-hour tape is complete. A later bar where price and CVD move the same way ends the run; flat bars after it do not. A 4-hour window that disagrees without a current run is in line, not a run. Prices come from Binance 5-minute candles, then Bybit if Binance has none. A missing price leaves this cell blank and still leaves day CVD in place.
- Print size: percent change of the 15-minute average fill versus the 15 minutes before it. This reads the in-memory tape, or stored flow-at-prices minutes when that tape does not cover the half hour. It does not walk public trade history.
- Order count: `(buy − sell) / (buy + sell)` over those same 15 minutes. Counts are summed only when every expected venue's window is complete and the newest print is still fresh. A venue with no fresh prints stays blank and is not counted as zero.
- Unusual: the farthest high or low reading versus that coin's own stored samples (funding, open-interest change, completed-hour liquidations, completed-day CVD, or hourly spread). The cell names the venue. Venues are not averaged. A coin with no standout stays blank, including when its own history is still too short. Print size is not part of this scan.
- Liquidation heat: the hottest 5-minute grade versus that venue's own typical rate (`quiet`, `elevated`, `cascade`, `extreme`). It stays empty until the venue has been watched for 6 hours. When the venues disagree, the cell names the hottest one.
- Bid share: resting bid notional within 1% of mid, summed across fresh books.
- Recorded span: how far back stored open interest, the spot book, flow-at-prices, and liquidation prints go for that coin. The cell shows the longest span of each series. The title lists each venue.

A combined cell is empty unless every expected venue is fresh for that column. A missing or old venue is named on the row and is not treated as zero. `definite` on the board is true only when every row is complete. The page is `/derivatives`. It opens on 36 names, and the coin menu can ask for 100. Any column sorts, and a blank cell stays at the bottom. Clicking a row opens that coin's chart. The unusual reading on a standout row opens the combined coin situation in place, the same clauses as the coin page, loaded when the row opens. A row with no standout does not open that read. The heatmap still opens the coin. On the time grid, the coin name opens the chart and a cell lists each exchange. A Hyperliquid row opens the Binance coin chart, because Hyperliquid has no candle page. The positioning lines on that chart are where its open interest and funding are drawn.

`exchange=hyperliquid` fills the mark, open interest, and the hourly funding rate when that coin is listed. Open interest is the mark times coin size. The contracts are USDC-margined and are lined up with the USDT name, so BTC is BTCUSDT. A name that does not match exactly stays missing. Liquidation, crowd, tape, CVD, print size, order count, unusual, liquidation heat, and book columns stay blank and are named `unsupported`. They are not zeros and they are not a missing feed. Hyperliquid is not added into `exchange=all`. Its funding is hourly, so it is not averaged into the 8-hour combined rate. On the time grid, funding and open-interest change fill from stored samples. CVD and liquidation cells stay blank and name Hyperliquid. Open-interest history grows only while this process is sampling. Funding history is read back from Hyperliquid's settled hourly prints.

The same page has a time grid. Coins stay down the side. `range=24h` is one column per clock hour. `range=7d` is one column per UTC day. The series is funding, open-interest change, CVD, or liquidation notional, read from the archive rather than the live board. Funding is the latest settled rate in that cell, or the latest predicted rate when the cell has no settlement. Open interest is the percent change from the previous cell. CVD is aggressive buy minus sell, and an hour or day counts only when the stored minutes reach both edges and cover about three quarters of an hour or four fifths of a day. Liquidations are stored force-order notional. A venue that was watched for that cell and printed nothing contributes zero. A cell stays blank until every expected venue has it. `missing` names the venue that does not, and that venue is not counted as zero. The open column also stays blank when its newest sample is older than five minutes. Clicking a cell lists each expected exchange. An exchange with that column shows its own number, including a real zero. A missing exchange is named and is not zero. The combined number stays blank until every exchange is in.

The same page has a heatmap. Rows are the coins already on the board. One control picks the column that paints the cells: funding, open-interest change, crowd long, taker buy, top minus crowd, day CVD, price versus CVD, print size, order count, unusual distance, liquidation heat, or bid share. The largest distance from that column's neutral is first. A cell with no definite value stays blank and is not drawn as zero. Day CVD color is this coin against the largest day CVD on the screen, because the column is a notional. Price versus CVD uses 60 minutes as full strength: price up while CVD falls is one direction, the reverse is the other, and in line stays neutral. The other columns use a fixed scale, so a quiet hour does not paint every cell at full strength. There is no second route and no new request.

## How to verify

`go test` for `domain`, `service/market`, the derivatives handler, and `transport/mcp` grid map. Frontend: `DerivativesBoard/helpers.test.ts`, `DerivativesBoard/DerivativesBoard.test.tsx`, `DerivativesHeatmap/helpers.test.ts`, `DerivativesTimeGrid/helpers.test.ts`, `DerivativesTimeGrid.test.tsx`, and `DerivativesPage.test.tsx`.

Open `/derivatives`. The table lists the liquid perpetuals. A dash in a combined column means that venue set is not complete. On a row with an unusual standout, open that reading and confirm the combined situation clauses. A row without a standout has no situation control. Choosing one venue shows that venue's own numbers. Choosing Hyperliquid fills mark, open interest, and hourly funding, and leaves liquidation, tape, and book cells blank. Heatmap uses those same rows: pick a column, confirm a withheld cell stays blank, and confirm the strongest reading is first. Time shows the archive grid: switch the series and the span, confirm a cell with a missing venue stays blank, and confirm a watched quiet liquidation hour shows zero. Click a cell and confirm each exchange is listed: a venue with a sample shows its own number, and a missing venue is named and is not zero. The coin name still opens the chart.

## Limits

The page can list at most 100 USDT perpetuals, which is the recorder cap. It opens on 36. Choosing 100 ranks the recorded set, including order count and unusual, on this board. Open interest, funding, long/short, and the 1% perpetual book are live REST snapshots. The 24-hour open-interest change appends one stored sample when the live series stops short of that hour. The board response is cached about 20 seconds. Day CVD, the price-versus-CVD run, print size, and order count do not start a REST history walk: day CVD and the run share stored minutes plus the live book, and print size and order count use the in-memory tape or, when that tape does not cover the window, stored flow-at-prices minutes. Unusual reads the same stored samples as the unusual scan and does not merge venues. Liquidation heat needs about 6 hours of watching before a grade is shown. Taker buy share uses each venue's existing 1-hour window: Binance is the public taker series, Bybit is complete after this process has collected that hour, and OKX only has the newest 500 swap trades, so its hour is often incomplete. Liquidation totals are the prints this process has seen in the last 24 hours. The recorded column is stored history, not a live quote. Hyperliquid is mark, open interest, and hourly funding only. It is not a fourth tape, book, or liquidation feed, and it is not part of the combined total.
