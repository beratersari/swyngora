# Chart desk

## Goal

Show the book, the tape, and estimated liquidation pockets on the same candle a person is already looking at, and say how long that coin has actually been recorded.

## Behavior

On a crypto coin Overview chart:

- Resting bid and ask size sits behind the candles and follows the candles on screen. Up to 30 minutes is the live tape. A longer view uses the stored minute ladders, up to about 30 days. Green is bids, red is asks. Stronger color is more size.
- Stored liquidation prints sit behind those candles for 12h, 24h, 3d, 7d, 14d, 30d, or 90d. Gold through magenta is more notional. Longs are positions closed by a sell, shorts by a buy. Binance, Bybit, and OKX stay separate. Combined color is drawn only when all three were recorded. A quiet recorded column is a real zero and stays clear. The caption says the map is measured prints and names any venue that was not recorded. Source switches to the labeled leverage model, which uses each venue's own price and open interest and names the leverage mix. Prints are `GET /api/v1/market/liquidation-prints/heatmap`. The model is `GET /api/v1/market/liquidation-hunt/heatmap`.
- Open liquidation levels sit on the candles for the same range. A band stays where that slice of open interest was placed until a later candle trades through it. Solid is still open. Dashed is already traded through. The line under the candles is still-open long size minus still-open short size. Combined bands stay off the chart when any venue has no price and open interest, and the caption says the book is withheld. The same read is `GET /api/v1/market/liquidation-open-levels`.
- Buy and sell sit inside each candle when stored minutes cover that range (`GET /api/v1/market/flow-at-prices/candles`). Each row is one traded price. The left half is sell, the right half is buy. The busiest row is outlined, and a side at least 3× the other is outlined on that half. Numbers show when the candle is wide enough. The read follows the candles on screen, in 8-candle blocks, up to 400 candles and about 30 days. Binance minutes for the liquid list are filled from public aggregate trades, about 7 days. Combined is drawn only when Binance, Bybit, and OKX were all recorded. A missing venue is named, and the chart shows the page's venue. The open minute uses the live tape when that tape already covers it. Futures CVD and spot CVD are separate lines under the candles. Each line is named Futures or Spot and the venue, or Combined. Futures combined is drawn only when Binance, Bybit, and OKX each reach the window open. A minute one of them missed is left out of the sum, not filled with the others. Spot combined is drawn only when Binance and Bybit do. OKX has no spot CVD. A Coinbase or OKX page shows Binance spot CVD and says Binance. A missing venue is named and is not counted as zero. Whale and retail futures CVD sit on their own panes under that futures line, on the same day, 4-hour, click, and running anchors. A whale is one aggressive print of at least $100,000, as the venue reports it. Retail is every smaller print. A minute stored before print size was known stays on the total line. A later bar without size breaks the whale and retail lines instead of holding zero. Combined whale and retail are drawn only when every venue's bar in that step was sized. Otherwise the chart draws the page venue and names the other venues.
- When stored minutes do not fill the candles, the right edge stays the footprint for the visible window: buy versus sell at each price. Panning the chart picks the nearest flow window (5 minutes through 30 days).
- Bars just left of that right-edge footprint are the largest estimated long and short liquidation pockets. The dashed 10× / 25× / 50× / 100× lines stay. Those bars stay when the footprint is drawn inside the candles.
- A pane under CVD is crowd long percent versus top-trader long percent. Binance and OKX draw both. Bybit draws the account crowd only, and the caption says it publishes no top-trader ratio. The next pane is that top-minus-crowd gap since 00:00 UTC and since the current 4-hour open. A venue whose stored sample has not reached that open is named, and that line stays off.
- The next pane is stored USDT open interest, one line each for Binance, Bybit, OKX, and Hyperliquid when that venue has samples. Hyperliquid open interest is the mark times coin size and starts only after this process has sampled it. The pane after that is the same open interest since 00:00 UTC and since the current 4-hour open. A venue that has not reached that open is named, and that line stays off. The next pane is stored funding percent, settled prints when that venue has them. Hyperliquid's line is the hourly rate. The next pane is liquidation notional in the same step as the other stored panes: short liquidations point up, long liquidations point down, and a step with both shows the difference. That histogram stays on Binance, Bybit, and OKX. Hyperliquid publishes no liquidation prints. The last pane is still-open long liquidation size minus still-open short liquidation size.
- Click a candle on a crypto chart and the forensics read for that open appears under the chart: the hour before, the next 15 minutes, and what similar setups did next (`GET /api/v1/market/forensics`). A similar row moves the read to that past time. Clear, or the same candle, removes it. Equities do not call it. The same click, while open interest, funding, or liquidation notional is on, restarts those three series at that candle (`GET /api/v1/market/positioning-anchor`). Open interest and funding draw the change from the last stored sample at or before the candle, one line per venue, including Hyperliquid when a sample exists. A venue with no sample by then is named and that line stays off. Liquidation notional is a running sum from that candle, short up and long down, one line per venue. Hyperliquid is not named as a missing liquidation feed. A print at or before the candle starts the sum at zero. A venue whose first print is later is named as late and the line starts at that print. The same click restarts CVD when that layer is on. Clicking the candle again, or Clear, removes it.
- A line under the chart names how long futures open interest, the spot book, flow-at-prices, and recorded liquidation prints have been stored on each venue. A venue with no samples is left out. Startup backfill covers the liquid set, up to 100 names, as far as each venue publishes.

The Book, Footprint, Crowd vs top, Open interest, Funding, Liquidations, Liq map, and Open levels switches turn those layers off. Footprint uses the in-candle rows when they exist, and the right-edge ladder otherwise. Range and venue show when either the map or the open levels are on. Side and source stay on the map. Equities do not get them. Crowd versus top, open interest, funding, and liquidation notional read `GET /api/v1/market/futures-history` with `bucket` matched to the loaded candles: 5 minutes through 24 hours, 30 minutes through 7 days, 2 hours through 30 days, and 4 hours after that. The window starts at the first loaded candle and stops at 90 days. A step with no stored sample is left out. A venue with no stored rows is left off that pane. The server does not fill months the exchanges do not publish.

The Desk control focuses one set of those layers. All keeps every layer on. Positioning turns on open interest, funding, crowd versus top, open liquidation levels, and the 10×–100× lines. Click a candle on that desk to restart open interest, funding, and liquidation notional from it. A line names that page venue's later-print hit rate and the share of the model size that was kept. Open bands stay until a later candle trades through them. The liquidation heatmap stays off on that desk. Order flow turns on buy−sell, the day and 4-hour futures CVD, whale and retail CVD when print size was stored, the day and 4-hour spot CVD, and the footprint, and writes absorption and print size under the chart. Click a candle while CVD is on and futures and spot CVD restart at that candle. The same click, or Clear, removes it. A candle older than the selected range loads the stored bars that reach it. While CVD is on, one circle marks the start of the current price-versus-CVD run on the futures venue whose line is drawn, and the caption names that venue, the duration, and any venue that starts later. A later bar where price and CVD move the same way clears the mark; flat bars after the run do not. Agreement draws no mark. Positioning and Liquidity leave the mark off. Liquidity turns on the book shading and writes perp bid share and the largest live walls on this venue. Combined absorption, print size, and perp bid share are written only when every venue covers the window. A missing venue is named and is not treated as zero. A manual layer switch leaves the desk unmatched. Equities do not get the control. These reads use the same endpoints as the chart layers and the Flow and Tape tabs.

## How to verify

Open `/markets/binance/BTCUSDT`. The overview chart opens on All: book shading, the liquidation heatmap, buy and sell inside each candle when stored minutes exist, the right-edge footprint when they do not, the liquidation bars, and the crowd strip when those series have data. Positioning, Order flow, and Liquidity each turn on one desk. Order flow names a venue whose absorption or print size does not cover the window. Liquidity names a venue that does not cover the perp band, and lists live walls on this venue's book. The map opens on measured prints. Source switches to the leverage model, and that caption names the mix. Open interest, funding, crowd versus top, and liquidation panes follow the loaded candles once stored rows exist. Bybit is named as a venue with no top-trader ratio. Open interest and the top-versus-crowd gap also draw the change since 00:00 UTC and since the current 4-hour open. Open levels draw the same two resets for still-open long size minus still-open short size, and the caption states the long and short changes. Click a candle and the card under the chart reads what changed around that open and what similar setups did next. The same click on Positioning restarts open interest, funding, and liquidation notional there. The same click restarts the still-open liquidation book. The caption names a venue that has not reached that candle. The caption names the futures recording span once a sample exists. A range longer than the loaded candles asks you to pan left.

`go test` for `domain`, `futureshist`, `bookhist`, `market`, and the history-span handler. Frontend: `chartDesk.test.ts`, `chartPresets.test.ts`, `DetailChartToolbar.test.tsx`, `CoinCandleForensics.test.tsx`, and `CoinDetailPage.test.tsx`.

## Limits

Open interest, funding, crowd versus top, and liquidation notional follow the loaded candles at 5-minute, 30-minute, 2-hour, or 4-hour steps, capped at 90 days. A missing step is omitted. Binance public open interest and long/short still cover about 30 days, and OKX hourly history is the latest 1,440 bars, so a longer chart stays empty on the left where those venues never published a sample. The caption names a venue whose open interest or top-versus-crowd gap has not reached 00:00 UTC or the current 4-hour open. Book shading follows the visible candles and uses stored ladders past 30 minutes, up to about 30 days, not years of depth. The liquidation map behind the candles uses the same 12h–90d grid as `/liquidations` and opens on measured prints. On the model, a range past a day reads one stored open-interest sample per step, and a column with no sample stays empty. Open levels use that same window and stay at their birth price. The still-open long-minus-short line also resets at 00:00 UTC, at the current 4-hour open, and at a clicked candle. A reset stays off when the stored book starts later, and a missing venue keeps the combined reset off. The model map still reprices the whole book on every column. Open levels and the model map are the leverage model. Prints are forced closes this process stored. `/liquidations?view=open` is the same open-level read. The first paint loads 300 candles, about 12 days on the default 1-hour interval, so 30-day and 90-day columns sit off the left until you pan. That first view keeps the latest candle on the right after open interest, funding, and the open-level line add times that fall between candles. The candle footprint keeps at most 24 price rows and 400 candles. A wider view keeps the newest candles. A venue with no stored minutes is named and is not treated as zero. The liquidation pockets beside the candles are that same model. The wider recorder samples about 100 USDT perpetuals. Binance books outside the seed list use the top 100 levels and are snapshotted about every 2 minutes. Flashes stay on the seed pairs and coins someone opened. Bybit and Coinbase snapshot the wider list every minute. Bybit's liquidation socket follows that same 100-name list. Startup backfills public futures history for that same liquid list. Recorded liquidation prints stay with the futures archive (about 90 days). Public refill is still about 7 days.
